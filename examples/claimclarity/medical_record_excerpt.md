# Riverside Physical Therapy Group — Initial Evaluation Note (excerpt)

**Patient:** Maria Chen
**Date of Visit:** June 15, 2026
**Referring complaint:** 3 weeks of low back pain, no radiation into the legs,
aggravated by prolonged sitting and improved by movement.

## Assessment

Lumbar active range of motion mildly limited in flexion. Positive prone
instability test. Pain reproduced and increased with lumbar extension,
relieved with flexion-based positioning. No red flag symptoms (no saddle
anesthesia, no bowel/bladder changes, no unexplained weight loss). Clinical
presentation and exam findings are consistent with **vertebrogenic low back
pain**, not a nonspecific presentation.

## Plan

Begin therapeutic exercise program (CPT 97110), 2x/week for 6 weeks, focused
on core stabilization and extension-based mobility work. Home exercise
program provided.

At the end of today's visit, the patient specifically requested a 15-minute
relaxation massage as an add-on; this was provided as a self-pay amenity and
was not clinically indicated as part of the treatment plan for her back pain.
