# Heartland Mutual Health Plan — Summary of Benefits (excerpt)

**Member:** Maria Chen (HMH-88231045)
**Plan Year:** January 1 – December 31, 2026

## Outpatient Rehabilitative Therapy
Physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech-language therapy are
**covered services**, combined limit of 20 visits per plan year, 20%
coinsurance after the annual deductible is met. Prior authorization is not
required for the first 12 combined visits in a plan year.

As of June 15, 2026, Maria Chen had used 3 of her 20 combined visits this
plan year; the June 15 visit was her 4th.

## Massage Therapy
Massage therapy is **not covered under any circumstance**, including when
billed in conjunction with a covered rehabilitative therapy visit, regardless
of medical necessity or physician order. Members may pay for massage therapy
out of pocket at participating providers.

## General Notes
Claims must be submitted with a diagnosis code that is specific and valid for
billing purposes; category-level diagnosis codes (e.g. an unqualified code
without a required 4th/5th character) are not accepted and will be returned
for correction — this is a billing/coding issue, not a coverage decision, and
does not require prior authorization or additional medical evidence to
resolve.
