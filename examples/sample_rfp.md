# Request for Proposal RFP-2026-014

## City of Rivertown — Parks & Public Grounds Maintenance Services

**Issuing Organization:** City of Rivertown, Department of Public Works
**Issue Date:** August 3, 2026
**Project Title:** Annual Grounds Maintenance Services for Rivertown Municipal Parks

### 1. Overview and Scope of Work

The City of Rivertown is seeking proposals from qualified landscaping and
grounds maintenance contractors to provide year-round mowing, trimming,
irrigation upkeep, seasonal planting, and tree/shrub care for eleven (11)
municipal parks totaling approximately 84 acres. The contract term is 24
months with two optional 12-month renewals at the City's discretion. Services
must be performed weekly during the growing season (April–October) and
biweekly the remainder of the year.

### 2. Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for award, bidders MUST:

- Be licensed to do business in the state and hold a current Landscape
  Contractor's License issued by the state licensing board.
- Have a minimum of 3 years of continuous operation providing commercial or
  municipal grounds maintenance services.
- Hold a current state Pesticide Applicator Certification for any bidder
  intending to apply herbicides or pesticides on City property.
- Carry the insurance coverage described in Section 3.
- Not be currently debarred from bidding on any local, state, or federal
  contracts.

### 3. Required Insurance

Awarded contractor must carry and maintain, for the duration of the contract:

- Commercial General Liability insurance with a minimum of $2,000,000 per
  occurrence and $4,000,000 aggregate.
- Automobile Liability insurance with a minimum of $1,000,000 combined single
  limit.
- Workers' Compensation insurance meeting statutory state requirements.
- The City of Rivertown must be named as an additional insured on the General
  Liability policy.

### 4. Evaluation Criteria

Proposals will be scored out of 100 points as follows:

- Technical approach and staffing plan — 30 points
- Relevant past performance / references (minimum 3 references from
  contracts of similar size in the last 5 years) — 25 points
- Pricing — 25 points
- Certified Arborist on staff (ISA certification preferred but not required)
  — 10 points
- Sustainability practices (e.g., integrated pest management, native
  planting, water conservation) — 10 points

### 5. Submission Requirements

- Proposals must be submitted electronically as a single PDF to
  bids@rivertown.gov.
- Maximum length: 15 pages, excluding required attachments and insurance
  certificates.
- Font: 11pt or larger, single-spaced.
- Proposals must include:
  1. Signed cover letter
  2. Technical approach and staffing plan
  3. Minimum of 3 client references with contact information
  4. Copy of current Landscape Contractor's License
  5. Copy of current Pesticide Applicator Certification (if applicable)
  6. Certificate of Insurance evidencing coverage described in Section 3
  7. Completed pricing schedule (Attachment A)
  8. Signed non-collusion affidavit (Attachment B)

### 6. Submission Deadline

All proposals must be received no later than **5:00 PM Eastern Time on
September 30, 2026**. Late submissions will not be considered under any
circumstances.

### 7. Questions

Questions must be submitted in writing to procurement@rivertown.gov no later
than September 12, 2026.
